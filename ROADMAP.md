# Roadmap

Ideas that are agreed in principle but not built yet.

## Next

- **Share tracks online:** the host's track is sent to the guest automatically, so
  both players no longer need it installed. This also makes editor tracks more
  worth building.
- **Rematch button:** race again from the results screen without leaving the room.
- **3-4 players online:** a real relay change, because a room is strictly a host
  plus one partner today.

## Later

- **More race types:** Elimination (last place goes out every lap) uses the
  existing AI and race rules. Sprint (point to point) also needs track work,
  because every track is a closed loop today.
- **Career ladder:** a chain of events that earn cash or stars, which unlock cars
  and paint. It gives players a reason to come back.
- **Cop chases:** an AI pursuit mode with roadblocks and a heat level. It would be
  the game's signature feature, and it's the most work here, because cop AI needs
  a lot of tuning.
- **Ranked play and profiles:** accounts, ranked races and persistent profiles.
  These need the relay deployed with abuse protection first.

## Not planned

- **Weather and tyre wear:** they push towards the realism the handling has moved
  away from.
- **Car upgrade trees:** not until the career exists.
- **Drift scoring and a Drift mode:** tried and dropped on 2026-11-01. Sliding
  earns nitro, and that's enough.
