# Roadmap

Ideas that are agreed in principle but not built yet.

## Later

- **More race types:** Elimination (last place goes out every lap) uses the
  existing AI and race rules. Sprint (point to point) also needs track work,
  because every track is a closed loop today.
- **Career ladder:** a chain of events that earn cash or stars, which unlock cars
  and paint. It gives players a reason to come back.
- **Cop chases:** an AI pursuit mode with roadblocks and a heat level. It would be
  the game's signature feature, and it's the most work here, because cop AI needs
  a lot of tuning.
- **Matchmaking with strangers:** a button that puts you in a room with other
  players, with no code to share. The relay would fill open rooms on the same
  version and start them after a short wait, on a built-in track. It fits v3 as a
  new relay event and a `FEATURES` step. Strangers can't be trusted the way
  friends can, so it needs a kick or vote-out, and cheating stays possible until
  ranked play brings a server that checks results.
- **Ranked play and profiles:** accounts, ranked races and persistent profiles.
  These need a server that checks results, because clients are trusted today.
- **Freestyle drift counter:** a combo multiplier for chaining slides, a saved
  best per car, and a pop when a combo is banked. This is only freestyle's
  existing counter: Drift mode and drift scoring in races stay not planned.
- **More live changes in freestyle:** time of day and handling from the pause
  menu, as car and paint already are.

## Not planned

- **Weather and tyre wear:** they push towards the realism the handling has moved
  away from.
- **Car upgrade trees:** not until the career exists.
- **Drift scoring and a Drift mode:** tried and dropped on 2026-11-01. Sliding
  earns boost, and that's enough.
