# SPPPD

A top-down racing game built with [LÖVE](https://love2d.org) 11.5. The handling is
built on a tyre and vehicle model, tuned for arcade fun by default. Everything you
see and hear is generated by the game, and freestyle will play your own music.

Its website, with downloads, the changelog and a blog, is
<https://spppd.vanillaiice.xyz>.

    make run

Race the AI, set time trials, drift around in freestyle, build your own tracks, or
race up to three friends online. There are 12 cars, 10 tracks and four times of day.

## Controls

| Action | Keys | Pad (Xbox / PlayStation) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steer | A D / arrows | Left stick |
| Accelerate, brake and reverse | W S / arrows | RT LT / R2 L2 |
| Handbrake (drift on Arcade) | Shift | RB / R1 |
| Nitro on/off | Space | LB / L1 |
| Reset to the track | R | Y / Triangle |
| Headlights (low, high) | F, G | |
| Change camera | C | Back / Share |
| Shift gears in manual | Q, E | X A / Square Cross |
| Next song in freestyle | N | D-pad right |
| Horn | H | B / Circle |
| Pause | Esc or P | Start / Options |

At the start lights, hold the revs on the white bar for a good launch.

**Touch** controls appear on phones and tablets: a stick under your left thumb, DRIFT
and BOOST on the right, and horn, reset and pause at the top right. The car accelerates
by itself unless you turn Auto Gas off.

## Good to know

- **Fences** bite: a hard hit wrecks the car, which costs you over a second and all your
  speed. Glancing off one is fine, and so is being shoved into one by another car.
- **Handling** is set in race setup. Arcade is the default, with big grip and a held
  drift on the handbrake. Sport adds realistic grip with driver aids, and Sim has no
  aids at all. Online races always use Arcade.
- **Nitro** fills on its own, and three times faster while the car is sliding.
- **Slipstream**: sit close behind a car at speed for less drag, then pull out and
  slingshot past.
- **Pace notes** at the top of the screen show the next corner, how tight it is (1 is a
  hairpin, 4 a fast kink) and when to brake.
- **Radio**: freestyle plays MP3, OGG, FLAC or WAV files. Drop them on the game window,
  or use MUSIC FOLDER in freestyle setup. Not on phones.

## Track editor

Open it from the main menu, on a desktop. Click to add a node, drag to move it, right
click to delete it, and select one to change its width. Press **T** to drive the track
at any time. Kerbs, barriers and the racing line are worked out for you.

## Online

Two to four players race through a relay server. One player picks HOST A ROOM, sets the
track, laps and time of day, and reads out the room code. The others enter it and join,
and only the host needs the track. REMATCH runs the race again once everyone asks.

Everyone needs the same version of the game. The ONLINE screen lists what else to
expect: it's new, you can't rejoin after leaving, and nobody checks results.

What the official server keeps is in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md), and its rules are in
[TERMS.md](TERMS.md). PRIVACY & TERMS on the ONLINE screen opens them.

To run your own relay, open UDP port 8080 and have everyone set SERVER to your address:

    make server
    ./dist/relay -addr :8080 -dashboard 127.0.0.1:9090

The dashboard at <http://localhost:9090> shows rooms and traffic, and can close rooms
and ban IPs. Bans and dashboard users are kept in `spppd-relay.db` (`-db`). Until you
add a user, the dashboard only answers on this machine; with one, it takes a login from
anywhere:

    ./dist/relay user add NAME

`-max-players` (2000) and `-max-rooms` (no limit) cap how many people and rooms the
relay takes at once, and the dashboard shows both against their limits.

Statistics are off unless you start the relay with `-analytics`. They count unique IPs
as keyed hashes, plus rooms, races and traffic, and the dashboard exports them as CSV.
If you turn them on, tell your players, as `PRIVACY.md` does for the official server.
`make deploy-relay` deploys to the hblabs server. See `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Building and testing

    make test                 # simulation, rules, editor, audio, about 8 minutes
    make test-visual          # the real game through its input handlers
    make test-server          # the relay
    make test-online          # clients through a real relay
    make test-online-visual   # the real game online, against bots
    make zip win64 win32      # release builds
    make apk                  # a test build for Android
    make site                 # the website, after the release builds
    make deploy-site          # the website, deployed to spppd.vanillaiice.xyz

`ARCHITECTURE.md` explains how the game works, `REVIEW.md` what falls short, `SOUND.md`
how it's meant to sound, and `ROADMAP.md` what's next.
