SPPPD 4.0 is out. The [changelog](/changelog/) lists everything in it. This is the
story behind the bigger parts.

4.0 still races 3.x online, so nobody has to update at the same time as their friends.

## Tracks from other players, in the game

Since 3.7, [spppd.xyz/tracks](/tracks/) has had tracks made by players, but getting
one meant copying a code from the website and pasting it into the editor. Now TRACKS
ONLINE on the main menu lists them in the game, with each one's outline, length and
maker. GET saves one, and it's ready in RACE, TIME TRIAL and the editor. It works the
same on a phone.

Made a track you're proud of? SEND ONLINE in the editor sends it in, with your name
on it. It shows up in the list once it's been checked.

If you run your own server, it serves its own track list, and the game finds it from
the SERVER setting, which is now in SETTINGS as well as on the ONLINE screen.

## Menus made for thumbs

The phone version is the most downloaded, and its menus still felt like they were
made for a mouse. A tap fired the moment your finger landed, with no sign of what you'd hit and
no way to change your mind. Now a row lights up while you hold it and only fires when
you lift, so sliding off cancels it. RESET in a race works the same way, so a stray
thumb doesn't send you back to the track.

Rows are bigger, rows with choices have < and > buttons to tap, and BACK is always in
the same spot at the top of the screen. Race setup keeps START at the bottom of every
page, and ONLINE puts the ways in first.

The menus and the race also use the whole screen again, keeping clear of only the
camera notch, and the notes on ONLINE no longer run into the car beside them.

## Quick race, different every time

QUICK RACE used to start the same race every time. Now it picks its own track and
car: once when the game opens, and again after every race you finish, never the same
track twice in a row. RACE keeps the track and car you set there.

## A new engine underneath

SPPPD now runs on LÖVE 12. The main reason is HTTPS, which TRACKS ONLINE needs to
fetch tracks safely. The handling, the barriers and the look are all as before.

It brings a few changes with it. There are proper downloads for Linux (an AppImage)
and macOS (an app). 32-bit Windows is gone, as LÖVE 12 doesn't support it, and Android
now needs 7.0 or later. On a phone, the game goes quiet when you switch to another
app and picks up where it was when you come back. The first launch after updating
builds the sounds again, once.

## What's next

The [roadmap](/roadmap/) has the plans. More phone improvements are on the way, and
4.x updates keep racing 3.x online.
